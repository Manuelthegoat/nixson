Standards

What we commit to

We tell you when there is no case

Declining work we cannot usefully do is the main thing that separates this practice from the industry's worst end.

We do not approach victims

No cold calls, no unsolicited emails, no direct messages. Every client comes to us or is referred to us.

We separate fact from inference

Our reports state what the evidence establishes and what it merely suggests, and label which is which.

We work inside the law

No unauthorised access to systems, no impersonation, no contact with the people who defrauded you.

Ask us anything before you commit

Including the uncomfortable questions. Especially those.
